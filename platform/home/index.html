01 / How it works

One profile for every site you're on.

If you have your own site and you're on a podcast's site too, both run on your one ComicTable profile. Update your dates or bio there and both sites change.

  1. Add the showPick the club, paste the ticket link. Showtimes fill themselves in.
  2. Both sites list itYour own site and your podcast's site each show it, in their own design.
  3. See who's nearbyFans sign up by city. You see how many live near each show.

02 / What it runs

Turn on what you need.

Everything is edited from one dashboard. Comics, co-hosts and their teams each get their own login.

2.1

Shows

A weekend of dates in about a minute, with ticket links and sold-out labels.

2.2

Show finder

Give it a club's calendar page. When a show with your name goes up there, it lands in your dashboard to add in one click.

2.3

Podcast and YouTube

New episodes and uploads post themselves.

2.4

Fan list

Sign-ups by city, so you know who to tell when you're in town.

2.5

Forum and chat

A room for your fans, with your own moderators.

2.6

Blog and merch

Posts for the bits that didn't make the set, and a shelf for the shirts.
