The problem

Link in bio is not a website.

Comics are running a small business out of their notes app. Keeping fans up to date shouldn't eat a weeknight.

Dates live in five places

Instagram, the club's site, a Linktree, a Google Doc, the group chat. Nobody's sure which is current.

Every show has its own link

Three shows a weekend means three ticket links, and they're never named anything useful.

The followers aren't yours

An app decides who sees your post about Thursday. Your fans should hear from you directly.

Get started

Three ways onto ComicTable.

Same engine and same dashboard, whichever you pick. You can move from one to another later without losing a thing.

Fastest

Start from a template

Pick a design, add your name, photo and colors, and connect your podcast. You're live the same day, on your own web address.

Most yours

Get one designed for you

We build your front end with you, from scratch. A 90s cantina with a chicken, or clean and minimal. Nobody else's site looks like it.

Keep your site

Plug it into the site you have

On Squarespace, Wix, WordPress or something custom? Paste a snippet, and your shows, fan sign-up and episodes stay up to date from the dashboard.
