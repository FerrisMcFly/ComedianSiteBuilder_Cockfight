## Logo rules

- **Name:** always "ComicTable", one word, capital C and T. Never "ComicTable.com" in the logo.
- **No tagline.** None is approved. Don't put "Less admin. More stage time." anywhere.
- **Wordmark and mark** work together or apart. On a page with the wordmark in the header, use the mark only for small spots: favicon, avatar, footer.
- **Clear space:** keep at least the height of the "o" (about 40% of cap height) clear around the wordmark. For the mark, keep 25% of its width clear.
- **Minimum size:** wordmark 96px wide on screen or 25mm in print. Mark 16px on screen (use the favicon files below 24px) or 6mm in print.
- **Backgrounds:** ivory, white, or charcoal. On photography, use the one-color ivory version over a dark, quiet area only.
- **Don't:** stretch, outline, add shadows or gradients, swap the two colors, set it in another font, add a microphone, stool or table, or put it in a badge.

## Color

| Token | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Charcoal | `#1F1F1F` | Primary text and dark fields |
| Redwood | `#C4452E` | Core accent, "Table", calls to action. For emphasis, not wall-to-wall |
| Mustard | `#E0A21A` | Occasional secondary accent: a small tag or a rule. Never body text |
| Ivory | `#F3EAD7` | Primary light background |

Derived states and contrast notes are in `tokens.css`. White on redwood is 4.96:1. Redwood text on ivory is only for large type. Small redwood text uses `#A93A26`.

## Type

- **Display and logo:** a heavy, compact late-70s corporate face in the spirit of the board's Albertus reference. The wordmark is outlined from **Encode Sans Condensed Black** (SIL Open Font License, free for commercial use and embedding). The site's headlines use the same family (700 to 900), self-hosted from `/fonts/`. If you license ITC Albertus or a custom cut later, the wordmark stays as-is and only the headline font changes.
- **Body and UI:** Inter (SIL Open Font License).

## Layout and imagery

- Editorial grid with thin charcoal rules, numbered section labels (`01 / How it works`), large headlines and generous ivory space, balanced by full charcoal fields.
- Flat color. Period texture appears in photography only.
- Photography: real black-and-white or muted documentary shots of comedy rooms, stages, mics and stools. No neon, sci-fi, stock-comedy tropes or VHS filters.
- The dashboard stays modern, plain and accessible: brand colors for accents, Inter throughout, light and neutral dark modes.
- Comics' own sites keep their own branding. ComicTable's identity is for the platform and its sales materials.
