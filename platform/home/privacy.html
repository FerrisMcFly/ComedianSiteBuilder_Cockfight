ComicTable

Privacy

The short version: you join a comic's list, they can tell you when they have a show near you. That's it.

What we keep

  • Your name, email and ZIP code.
  • Where you signed up (a comic's site, their Coaster or a QR code), and "where we met" if you filled it in.

Who sees it

The comic whose list you joined, and the people on their team. If you join a show's or group's list, the comics on that lineup when you sign up get you on their lists too. Nobody else.

What it's for

Telling you about shows within about 50 miles of your ZIP. We don't sell it, rent it or share it with advertisers.

Getting off the list

Every email has a one-tap unsubscribe link. One tap takes you off every list you joined through ComicTable.

Deleting everything

Want your email gone completely? Email hello@comictable.com from that address and we'll delete it.
